# OpenPaymentExpectation - Standard Document v1.0.0
**Free to use. Expected to pay.**

A voluntary payment standard for open-source software: organizations that benefit from it are expected to pay.

## 1. Who is NOT expected to pay
No payment is expected for personal or hobbyist use; education, teaching or academic research; nonprofits, charities and community groups; organizations with fewer than 20 full-time-equivalent workers or less than US$2 million annual revenue. Regardless of organization size, software development people are exempt: programming, testing, QA, business analysis, project and product management, design, DevOps, technical documentation, maintenance and open-source development. Regardless of organization size, exempt is trying out, evaluating, demonstrating or testing the software before deciding whether to adopt it. We welcome payment from anyone who wants to support this project financially, even if we don't expect it.

## 2. Who is expected to pay
Any other organization using the software, including businesses, government departments and public-sector bodies, is expected to pay. Any organization that uses this software to provide an operational or production service is expected to pay.

## 3. What we expect you to do
We expect you to ask your organization to pay, and to take reasonable steps to get this expectation to someone who can approve payment. If you can approve it, arrange payment through your organization's normal process. If you cannot, send the payment link below to your manager, finance, procurement or whoever authorizes software spending. A reasonable effort is enough: you are not expected to circumvent policy, pursue a rejected request indefinitely, or pay personally.

## 4. Expected, not legally required
This is not a license or contract. It is published separately from the project's open-source license, which remains authoritative where the two appear to conflict. Paying is the normal and responsible thing to do - we expect you to pay if you use this software.

## 5. Payment and recognition
Organizations may choose to be publicly recognized as an **OpenPaymentExpectation Organizational Contributor**. 

## 6. Adoption
Any genuinely open-source project may adopt this standard; no permission, registration, fee or membership is required. Any user of this document must add their own payment URL but otherwise keep the wording intact.

## 7. Forking
This expectation for you to pay remains true even for open source forks of this project.
